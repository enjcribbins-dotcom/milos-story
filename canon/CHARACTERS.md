# Guardians of the Elements — Characters

## Milo

**Role:** Protagonist  
**Age at opening:** 11

Milo is a human boy who lives in a cabin near the forest with Black Paw. He is curious, brave and closely connected to the natural world.

### Established abilities
- Flight and hovering.
- Nature/plant control limited to plants near him.
- Water control requiring nearby water.
- Excessive use of his powers causes exhaustion.

### Origin of powers
Milo inherited a small amount of potential from his parents. After their deaths, the dragons help him develop and form that potential into usable powers.

Exact mechanics remain open.

## Black Paw

**Role:** Milo's closest companion and protector  
**Species:** Wolf

Black Paw is explicitly a wolf, not a person or humanoid character, and does not wear armour.

He knew Milo when Milo was young. When Milo's parents died, Milo was about two and Black Paw was about seven. Black Paw and the forest largely raised Milo.

Black Paw keeps a secret connected to Milo's parents: a journal they entrusted to him. He has promised to protect it until Milo is ready.

The exact contents, hiding place and message of the journal remain open.

## Ember

**Role:** Dragon companion

Ember is found as an egg by Milo and Black Paw. The egg hatches and Ember joins their team.

Ember is brave, loyal and a close friend to Milo.

The recovered standalone story also contains an older version in which a dragon named Ember is found trapped beneath a rock. That version is preserved in recovery archives but conflicts with the Book 1 sequence in which Ember is discovered as an egg. It is therefore source material, not current working canon.

## Umbra

**Role:** Primary antagonist  
**Element/force:** Shadow

Umbra is the central enemy connected to the ancient Guardian conflict and to the mystery surrounding Milo's parents.

The exact fate of Umbra after the Ancient Guardian War is unresolved.

## Nova

Nova begins as a rival and eventually becomes an ally. This direction was explicitly selected in the recovered material.

Further details remain to be reconciled.

## Terragorn

Terragorn was formerly allied with Umbra and later became independent. This direction was explicitly selected.

Further details remain open.

## Inferno

Inferno is an ancient fire beast, explicitly **not a dragon**. Inferno is independent rather than a member of the Shadow Legion.

Further details remain open.

## Voltaris and Glaciera

Both are associated with the wider elemental conflict in the historical planning. Their exact allegiance and role remain unresolved.

## Goblin Tribe

The Goblin Tribe is an independent faction, explicitly separate from the Shadow Legion.

Named members:
- Grizzle — Goblin King
- Sparks — Goblin Engineer
- Mossnose — Goblin Scout

Their culture, goals and relationship to the main story remain open.

## Shadow Legion

The Shadow Legion has a mixed structure: Umbra has a loyal core, while some other powers may be allies or independent forces rather than obedient members.

Exact membership and hierarchy remain unresolved.
