# Canon Decisions

This file records decisions made after comparing recovered source material.

## 2026-10-04 — First reconciliation pass

### Promoted to working canon

1. **Book 1 title:** *Guardians of the Elements*.
2. **Book structure:** the user-established 20-chapter sequence is the working Book 1 structure.
3. **Milo:** eleven years old at the opening; protagonist.
4. **Black Paw:** Milo's closest companion; a real wolf, not a humanoid character and not wearing armour.
5. **Ember:** a dragon discovered as an egg by Milo and Black Paw; the egg hatches and Ember joins the team.
6. **Verdant and Azuren:** Guardian dragons who are already companions when encountered.
7. **Milo's powers:** small potential inherited from his parents; dragons help him develop it.
8. **Water limitation:** Milo needs nearby water to use water powers.
9. **Nature limitation:** Milo can only control nearby plants.
10. **Exhaustion:** excessive use of Milo's powers exhausts him.
11. **Parents:** Milo's parents were adventurers and died when he was about two.
12. **Black Paw's age at that time:** about seven.
13. **Milo's upbringing:** Black Paw and the forest largely raised him.
14. **Home:** Milo begins in a cabin near the forest.
15. **Parents' journal:** Black Paw protects a journal from Milo's parents and is meant to reveal it when Milo is ready.
16. **Nova:** begins as a rival and later becomes an ally.
17. **Terragorn:** formerly allied with Umbra, now independent.
18. **Inferno:** an independent ancient fire beast, explicitly not a dragon.
19. **Goblin Tribe:** independent from the Shadow Legion.
20. **Relics:** eight elemental relics, one for each major element/dragon, are part of the historical story framework.
21. **Ancient War:** the Guardians and Umbra fought an ancient war before Milo's birth; the relics existed then and were hidden afterward.

### Deliberate non-decisions

The following are **not** promoted to settled canon yet:

- Exact physical appearance of characters where the recovered text says the details were assistant-generated.
- Exact powers of secondary characters and dragons.
- Exact relic powers.
- Exact membership and hierarchy of the Shadow Legion.
- Exact roles/allegiance of Voltaris and Glaciera.
- Exact circumstances of Milo's parents' deaths, including whether Umbra personally killed them.
- Exact fate of Umbra after the Ancient Guardian War.
- Exact contents, hiding place and message of the parents' journal.
- Exact rules for portals.
- Exact ending/sequel hook.

### Important source conflict: Ember's first encounter

One recovered conversation contains a standalone story in which Milo finds Ember already hatched and trapped beneath a rock. Another recovered conversation establishes the Book 1 sequence in which Milo and Black Paw discover Ember as an egg, followed by Chapter 4 — *Ember Hatches*.

For the current **Book 1 working draft**, the egg/hatching sequence is used because it matches the user's explicit 20-chapter structure and later project planning.

The trapped-dragon story remains preserved in the recovered archives as historical source material and is not deleted or rewritten.

### Manuscript status

Chapters 1–4 are now working prose drafts assembled from the recovered evidence. They are intentionally editable and are not presented as immutable final canon.

The detailed 20-chapter outline remains historical planning material until the remaining chapters are drafted and reviewed.
