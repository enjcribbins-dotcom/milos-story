## Core story

**Book 1:** *Guardians of the Elements*

Milo is an eleven-year-old boy living in a cabin near the forest with his closest companion, Black Paw, a wolf who has known him since childhood. Milo's parents were adventurers who died when he was about two. Black Paw and the forest largely raised him.

Milo carries a small amount of elemental potential passed down from his parents. His powers are still developing, and the dragons help him learn to form and control them.

Milo can:
- fly and hover;
- influence nearby plants;
- use water powers only when water is nearby.

Using too much power exhausts him.

The story begins with Milo and Black Paw discovering a mysterious dragon egg. The egg hatches into Ember, who becomes part of their team. Later, they encounter the Guardian dragons Verdant and Azuren and learn that the present-day adventure is connected to an ancient conflict involving elemental relics and Umbra.
