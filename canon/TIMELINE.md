# Guardians of the Elements — Timeline

## Before Milo

- The elemental Guardians exist.
- An Ancient Guardian War is fought against Umbra.
- The elemental relics exist and are hidden.
- A long period of peace follows.

## Milo's early childhood

- Milo's parents are adventurers.
- Milo inherits a small amount of elemental potential from them.
- When Milo is about two, his parents die during an adventure connected to Umbra or Umbra's forces.
- Black Paw is about seven at this time.
- Black Paw and the forest largely raise Milo.
- Milo's parents' journal remains in Black Paw's keeping.

## Book 1

### Opening
Milo, age eleven, lives with Black Paw in a cabin near the forest.

### Early adventure
Milo and Black Paw discover strange tracks and follow them to a hidden cave.

They find a mysterious dragon egg.

The egg hatches and Ember joins them.

### Guardian discovery
The group travels into the Ancient Forest and meets Verdant and Azuren, who are already companions.

The heroes learn about the Ancient War and the elemental relics.

### Shadow threat
Signs of Umbra's return appear. A portal opens and the conflict escalates.

### Family revelation
Black Paw's secret and Milo's parents' journal become central later in the book.

### Final conflict
The historical outline builds toward Umbra's takeover, the heroes' comeback, a renewed portal opening and a final battle.

The exact final resolution and sequel hook remain draft material until reviewed.
