# Guardians of the Elements — World

## The setting

The story takes place in a valley surrounded by snowy mountains, with a forest close to Milo's home.

Milo begins in a cabin near the forest.

An Ancient Forest later becomes a major destination.

## Elemental Guardian Relics

The recovered story-bible session records the user's decision that there are **eight elemental relics**, one for each major element/dragon.

Historical elemental associations:

1. Nature — Verdant
2. Water — Azuren
3. Fire — Ember
4. Lightning — Voltaris
5. Ice — Glaciera
6. Earth — Terragorn
7. Star — Nova
8. Shadow — Umbra

The existence of eight relics and the one-per-major-element structure are working canon. Specific relic powers are not yet canon.

## The Ancient Guardian War

Long before Milo's birth, the Guardians and Umbra fought an ancient war.

The relics existed during that era and were hidden afterward. A long peace followed, and Milo's story takes place generations later.

The exact outcome of the war—especially whether Umbra was destroyed, defeated, imprisoned or escaped—is unresolved.

## Portals

Portals become important during Book 1.

They are central to:
- Chapter 11 — The Portal Opening
- Chapter 17 — The Reopening of the Portal

Their exact rules and origin remain open.

## Factions

### Guardians
Milo, Black Paw, Ember, Verdant and Azuren form the central heroic group. Nova later moves from rival to ally.

### Shadow forces
Umbra has a loyal core of shadow followers and may also work with independent or allied powers.

### Goblin Tribe
Independent from the Shadow Legion.

### Independent powers
Terragorn and Inferno are independent forces in the historical planning.

## Family mystery

Milo's parents were adventurers. They died when Milo was about two during an adventure connected, possibly, to Umbra or Umbra's forces.

Black Paw protects their journal and intends to reveal it when Milo is ready.

The exact circumstances of their deaths and the journal's contents remain unresolved.
