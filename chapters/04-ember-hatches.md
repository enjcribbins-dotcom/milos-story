# Ember Hatches

The egg trembled.

Milo and Black Paw stood perfectly still.

A thin crack ran across the shell.

Then another.

Milo swallowed.

“Something's inside.”

Black Paw sniffed the air.

The chamber was warm now. The strange symbols on the walls seemed brighter than before.

The egg rocked again.

A piece of shell fell away.

Something small pushed against the opening.

A tiny claw appeared.

Milo stared.

The claw disappeared.

Then appeared again.

The shell cracked wider.

A little snout pushed through.

Milo took a step back.

“Black Paw…”

The wolf stayed beside him, tense but watchful.

With one final crack, the top of the shell broke open.

A tiny dragon tumbled onto the moss.

It was red and gold, with small wings folded against its body.

For a moment, it lay perfectly still.

Then it sneezed.

A tiny flame burst from its nose.

Milo jumped.

The dragon blinked at him.

Milo smiled.

“Hello.”

The little dragon made a soft chirping sound.

Milo crouched beside him.

“You're a dragon.”

The dragon stretched his wings.

Black Paw lowered his head and sniffed him carefully.

The dragon sniffed back.

Milo laughed.

“I think he likes you.”

The dragon tried to stand.

His legs wobbled.

Milo held out his hands.

“Easy.”

The little dragon climbed into his arms.

He was warm.

Almost like holding a living ember.

Milo looked at Black Paw.

“We can't leave him here.”

Black Paw gave a quiet huff.

Milo smiled.

“That's what I thought.”

He wrapped the dragon carefully in the soft cloth from his pack.

The journey home was quiet.

The little dragon slept for most of the way.

Black Paw walked ahead.

Every few steps, Milo looked down.

The dragon was still there.

Still breathing.

Still real.

When they reached the cabin, Milo made a nest beside the fire.

The dragon opened his eyes.

“You're awake.”

A tiny flame flickered from his mouth.

Milo jumped.

The dragon sneezed.

Another puff of fire shot across the room.

Milo stared.

Black Paw ducked his head.

Milo began to laugh.

“Did you see that?”

The dragon looked almost embarrassed.

Milo crouched beside him.

“It's all right. We'll figure it out.”

The dragon sniffed the air.

Then he looked toward the fire.

Milo understood.

“You're hungry.”

He found some food and set it down.

The little dragon ate quickly.

Afterward, he curled beside the warmth.

Milo sat nearby.

“What should we call you?”

The dragon blinked.

Milo thought about the fire.

About the glow in the cave.

About the warmth he could still feel in his hands when he had first touched the egg.

“Ember,” he said.

The dragon lifted his head.

“Ember?”

The little dragon chirped.

Milo smiled.

“I think that's a yes.”

Black Paw settled beside the doorway.

For a while, the cabin was peaceful.

Milo watched Ember sleep.

He could not stop thinking about the symbols in the cave.

They had looked ancient.

Important.

And somehow connected to the same mysteries that surrounded his parents.

He looked at Black Paw.

“You knew there were things in the forest, didn't you?”

Black Paw did not answer.

“You know something about them.”

The wolf's golden eyes met Milo's.

For a moment, Milo thought Black Paw might finally speak.

Instead, Black Paw turned away.

Milo sighed.

“Fine. Keep your secrets.”

Ember stirred.

Milo reached down and gently touched the dragon's head.

Outside, the forest wind moved through the trees.

Deep beyond the cabin, something answered.

A distant roar.

Ember's eyes opened.

Black Paw stood.

Milo looked toward the dark window.

The sound came again.

Closer this time.

Milo felt a strange pull in his chest.

Whatever the dragon egg was connected to, the story had begun long before he found it.

And now Milo, Black Paw, and Ember were part of it.
