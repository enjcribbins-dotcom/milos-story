# The Last Battle

Milo ran toward the portal.

Ember flew beside him.

Black Paw followed.

Then Verdant appeared.

Azuren came with him.

Nova returned.

One by one, they gathered.

Umbra stood at the centre of the portal.

“You cannot defeat me,” he said.

Milo looked at him.

“Maybe not alone.”

The Guardians moved together.

Nature.

Water.

Fire.

Star.

Their powers met.

Not as separate weapons.

As one force.

Umbra's darkness pressed against them.

Milo raised the journal.

The ancient symbol on its cover blazed with light.

For an instant, Milo remembered his parents.

Not as ghosts.

As a memory.

A promise.

A reason to keep going.

Milo stepped forward.

“You don't get to decide what my family becomes.”

He reached toward the portal.

The darkness broke.

The portal began to close.

Milo held on.

Black Paw pushed against his side.

Ember cried out.

Milo remembered the forest.

The cabin.

The stream.

The first time he had seen the sky from Ember's back.

Then he let the power go.

The portal closed.

Silence fell.
