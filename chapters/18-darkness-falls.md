# Darkness Falls

The night seemed endless.

The Guardians were scattered.

Ember was somewhere above the clouds.

Verdant and Azuren were separated from the others.

Nova had disappeared into the mountains.

Milo stood beside Black Paw with the journal in his hands.

He opened the final page.

A faint line of writing appeared.

*Power is not what you can take.*

*It is what you choose to protect.*

Milo closed his eyes.

He understood.

His parents had not left him a weapon.

They had left him a choice.

The ground beneath him began to glow.

Plants rose through the cracks.

Water gathered from the mountain springs.

Milo lifted his hands.

The power did not feel like something he was forcing.

It felt like something he was listening to.

Black Paw stood beside him.

“I know what I'm supposed to do,” Milo said.

A familiar cry came from above.

Ember.

Milo looked up.

“Let's bring everyone home.”
