# Strange Tracks

Black Paw moved slowly through the forest.

Milo followed the trail behind him, careful not to disturb the strange impressions in the earth.

The tracks were unlike anything Milo had seen before.

They were too large for a wolf.

Too deep for a deer.

And they seemed to belong to something that had passed through the forest without making a sound.

Milo crouched beside another print.

“Black Paw, what do you think made these?”

The wolf lowered his nose to the ground.

He sniffed once.

Then again.

He continued forward.

Milo hurried after him.

The forest grew older as they travelled. The trees were wider, their roots twisting above the ground like the backs of sleeping giants.

The trail climbed steadily.

Milo began to notice something else.

The tracks were not random.

They were leading somewhere.

The trees thinned near the top of the slope, and Milo saw a dark opening in the rock ahead.

A cave.

Black Paw stopped at the entrance.

Milo stood beside him.

Cold air drifted from the darkness.

“I don't think that's an ordinary cave,” Milo said.

Black Paw gave a low growl.

Milo smiled nervously. “I know. That's why we're going in.”

Inside, the sound of the forest disappeared.

The cave walls were covered in marks.

Some looked like scratches. Others looked almost like symbols.

Milo ran his fingers over one of them.

For an instant, the stone seemed warm.

He pulled his hand back.

“Did you feel that?”

Black Paw stared at the wall.

Farther inside, something caught Milo's eye.

A faint glow.

They followed it around a bend.

The passage opened into a hidden chamber.

And there, resting on a bed of moss and old leaves, was an egg.

It was larger than Milo's head.

Strange lines curved across its shell, catching the pale light that came from somewhere above.

Milo forgot to breathe.

Black Paw stepped in front of him.

“It's all right,” Milo whispered. “I'm not going to touch it.”

The egg gave a tiny pulse of light.

Milo stared.

It pulsed again.

Something was alive inside.

Milo slowly moved closer.

“I think it knows we're here.”

Black Paw did not move.

Milo looked at the strange markings around the chamber.

He had never seen writing like it.

The egg pulsed once more.

Milo and Black Paw stared at it in silence.

Neither of them knew what they had found.

But Milo knew one thing.

They had discovered something that did not belong in an ordinary forest.
