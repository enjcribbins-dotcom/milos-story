# Meeting Verdant & Azuren

Milo did not know what to say.

Verdant and Azuren watched him from the edge of the clearing.

Ember stood between Milo and the two dragons, but he no longer looked frightened.

“You know Ember?” Milo asked.

Verdant nodded.

“We know what his egg represents.”

Milo looked down at Ember.

“What does it represent?”

Azuren answered.

“A history that was supposed to remain buried.”

The words made Milo uneasy.

Verdant looked toward the mountains.

“Long ago, the Guardians fought a war against a being of shadow.”

“Umbra,” Milo said.

The name felt strangely familiar.

Neither dragon answered immediately.

Finally Verdant nodded.

“Umbra.”

Milo's stomach tightened.

He thought of his parents.

“Did you know them?”

Verdant's expression changed.

“We knew of many adventurers who searched for the old relics.”

“Were my parents looking for them?”

Azuren looked at Verdant.

That silence told Milo enough to know there was more to the story.

“We cannot answer every question yet,” Azuren said.

Milo frowned.

“Why not?”

“Because knowledge can be dangerous when it arrives before someone is ready to carry it.”

Black Paw stepped forward.

Milo glanced at him.

For a moment, the wolf and the dragons seemed to understand one another without words.

Milo noticed.

“You knew about them too.”

Black Paw looked away.

Milo sighed.

“Everyone knows something except me.”

Verdant lowered his enormous head.

“That will change.”

The great dragon told them about the elemental relics.

There were eight.

Each was connected to a major element and to a Guardian force.

Nature.

Water.

Fire.

Lightning.

Ice.

Earth.

Star.

Shadow.

The relics had been hidden after the ancient war.

Milo listened carefully.

“Where are they now?”

“That is the question,” said Verdant.

Azuren looked toward the darkening sky.

“Because someone else has begun looking.”

The forest became silent.

A cold wind moved through the clearing.

Ember raised his head.

Black Paw's fur stood along his back.

Milo felt something he had never felt before.

Not fear exactly.

A warning.

Far away, beyond the trees, a patch of darkness appeared where no shadow should have been.

It flickered once.

Then vanished.

Verdant looked at Azuren.

“The shadow has stirred.”

Milo tightened his grip on his pack.

“What do we do?”

Verdant looked at him.

“You decide whether you are willing to learn the truth.”

Milo thought about his parents.

About Black Paw.

About Ember.

Then he looked at the place where the darkness had appeared.

“I am.”

The Ancient Forest seemed to hold its breath.

And somewhere beyond it, something was waking.
