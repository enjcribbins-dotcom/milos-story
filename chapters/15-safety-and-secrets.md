# Safety & Secrets

They found shelter in an abandoned stone hall beneath the mountains.

For the first time in days, there was quiet.

Milo looked at Black Paw.

“Tell me.”

The wolf walked to the far wall and pressed his paw against a loose stone.

It shifted.

Behind it was a narrow space.

Black Paw pulled out a worn journal.

Milo stared.

“You had this the whole time?”

Black Paw lowered his head.

Milo took the journal carefully.

The cover carried the same symbol he had seen in the cave.

Inside were his parents' names, maps, notes, drawings of dragons, and references to the elemental relics.

Milo read until the fire burned low.

His parents had known about the Guardians.

They had known about Umbra.

They had been searching for a way to protect the relics—and Milo.

Milo closed the journal.

“Did they know they were going to die?”

Black Paw looked away.

For the first time, Milo felt that his parents were not completely gone.

They had left him a path.
