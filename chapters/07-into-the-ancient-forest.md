# Into the Ancient Forest

They left the cabin at sunrise.

Milo carried food and water.

Black Paw carried nothing.

Ember carried himself.

He was very proud of that.

The map led them deeper into the forest than Milo had ever travelled.

The trees grew taller.

The paths disappeared.

Even the birdsong seemed different.

“This place feels old,” Milo said.

Black Paw stopped.

Ahead, a line of enormous stones rose from the earth.

Each stone carried the same markings they had seen in the cave.

Milo touched one.

A faint pulse travelled through his hand.

He pulled away.

The forest moved.

Not the trees.

The ground beneath them.

Roots shifted.

Branches bent.

A path opened where there had been none.

Ember chirped excitedly.

Milo looked at Black Paw.

“I'm guessing that's not normal.”

Black Paw started forward.

They followed.

The path led into a clearing.

At its centre stood a pool of clear water.

Milo felt the power inside it immediately.

He knelt beside the pool.

The water rose toward his hand.

He let it fall.

“I can feel something here.”

Ember looked toward the trees.

Black Paw's ears lifted.

A shadow moved between the trunks.

Milo stood.

“Who's there?”

No answer.

Then a shape stepped into the clearing.

It was enormous.

Milo had seen Ember fly.

He had seen the mountains.

But he had never seen a creature so large.

The dragon's scales were deep green.

Its presence seemed woven into the forest itself.

Milo slowly stepped backward.

Ember moved in front of him.

The great dragon lowered its head.

Then another dragon emerged beside it.

This one carried the colours of water and sky.

Milo stared.

Two ancient dragons.

Watching them.

The green dragon spoke first.

“You carry the mark.”

Milo looked at his hand.

“What mark?”

The dragon's eyes moved to the fragment of Ember's egg.

Then to Milo.

“The one you have not yet learned to see.”

Black Paw growled.

The water dragon remained calm.

“We are not your enemies.”

Milo looked from one dragon to the other.

“Then who are you?”

The green dragon lowered its head.

“I am Verdant.”

The blue-silver dragon stepped forward.

“And I am Azuren.”

Ember made a soft sound.

Verdant looked at him.

Recognition passed across the great dragon's face.

“You found him.”

Milo frowned.

“Found who?”

Verdant looked toward the ancient trees.

“The child of the old fire.”

The wind stopped.

Milo suddenly realised that the adventure had become much larger than a hidden egg.
