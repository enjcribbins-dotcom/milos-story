# Safety for Now

The next morning was quiet.

Milo read one more page of the journal.

It contained a simple instruction:

*Trust the ones who stand beside you.*

Milo looked around the shelter.

Ember slept beside the fire.

Azuren watched the entrance.

Verdant rested beneath the stone arch.

Nova sat apart from the others.

And Black Paw watched Milo.

The journal was not giving Milo every answer.

It was teaching him how to find them.

They spent the day preparing.

Milo practised small movements of water and learned not to push until his strength disappeared.

Ember practised flying through the mountain passages.

Nova taught Milo to watch for changes in the shadows.

For a few hours, they were safe.

Then the journal became warm.

A new mark appeared on its final page.

A circle.

The same shape as the portal.

The stone beneath them trembled.

Their safety was ending.
