# The Portal Opening

They had been travelling for three days when the sky changed.

Clouds gathered without warning. The air became cold.

Ember stopped.

Black Paw growled.

A dark line appeared across the sky. It widened until a doorway of darkness hung above the mountains.

Verdant turned sharply.

“The portal.”

Azuren raised a wall of water as strange creatures emerged from the opening.

Milo stepped forward.

“We can't let them reach the valley.”

The Guardians worked together. Verdant called roots from the earth. Azuren shaped the water into a barrier. Ember flew above them while Black Paw stayed beside Milo.

Milo reached toward the plants.

The roots twisted.

He pushed harder.

Pain shot through his arms.

Black Paw caught him.

“I’m okay,” Milo said.

Then the portal shook.

A voice came through the darkness.

Deep.

Cold.

Ancient.

“Milo.”

Verdant looked at him.

“Do not answer.”

The portal widened.

The enemy knew his name.
