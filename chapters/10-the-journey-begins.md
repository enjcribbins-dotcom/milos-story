# The Journey Begins

By sunrise, the decision had been made.

Milo, Black Paw and Ember would leave the Ancient Forest with Verdant and Azuren.

They needed answers.

Verdant showed Milo an ancient map.

Eight symbols were marked across it.

Nature.

Water.

Fire.

Lightning.

Ice.

Earth.

Star.

Shadow.

“The relics were hidden after the ancient war,” Verdant said.

Milo studied the map. “Can we find them?”

“We can try.”

They packed what they could carry.

For the first time, Milo looked back at the cabin from the edge of the forest.

It had been his whole world.

Now it looked smaller.

Ember nudged his hand.

Milo smiled. “Ready?”

The dragon chirped.

Black Paw started walking.

Milo followed.

The journey had begun.

Far behind them, where the portal had appeared, a whisper moved through the darkness.

“Milo.”
