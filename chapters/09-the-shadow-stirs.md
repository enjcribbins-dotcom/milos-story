# The Shadow Stirs

The darkness did not return all at once.

It began with the animals.

Birds stopped singing in the northern part of the Ancient Forest. Deer disappeared from familiar paths. Even the insects seemed to avoid certain clearings.

Verdant noticed first.

“The forest is afraid,” he said.

Milo looked around. “How can a forest be afraid?”

“When something old wakes,” Verdant said, “everything living feels it.”

Azuren stood beside the pool. The water had become strangely still.

Ember gave a low growl.

Black Paw's ears lifted.

Then a bird fell from the branches.

Milo ran to it. There was no wound, but a thin stain of darkness marked its feathers.

“Umbra?” Milo asked.

Verdant did not answer.

That night, a dark circle gathered between the trees.

A portal.

It flickered once, then disappeared.

Azuren stared at the empty space.

“The shadow has found a doorway.”

Milo tightened his grip on his pack.

“Then we need to find out where it leads.”
