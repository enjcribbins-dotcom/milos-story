# The Takeover

The first village they reached was already under Umbra's influence.

Smoke rose above the rooftops.

People were leaving by the road.

Verdant and Azuren hurried forward to help.

Ember flew over the buildings, searching for anyone who needed help.

Milo pulled a fallen beam away from a doorway using the plants growing beside it.

His arms began to shake.

He stopped before he used too much power.

Across the village square was a dark symbol.

It matched the marking from the cave.

“The relics,” Milo said. “Umbra is searching for them.”

That night, the group made camp outside the village.

“We cannot be everywhere,” Azuren said.

“Then where do we go?” Milo asked.

Verdant looked toward the mountains.

“Where the relics began.”

Milo looked at Black Paw.

The wolf's eyes were full of something Milo had seen before.

Guilt.

“What aren't you telling me?” Milo asked.

Black Paw turned away.

For now, the question would have to wait.
