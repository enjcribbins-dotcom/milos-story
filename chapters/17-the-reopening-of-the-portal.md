# The Reopening of the Portal

The portal opened beneath the mountain.

This time it did not flicker.

It tore through the stone and filled the chamber with darkness.

“Everyone out!” Milo shouted.

They reached the open air.

The sky was already dark.

Umbra had found them.

Verdant and Azuren took to the sky.

Nova stood beside Milo.

Ember flew overhead.

Black Paw remained at Milo's side.

The portal widened until it filled the valley.

Milo opened the journal.

A final map appeared.

It showed the centre of the portal.

Beneath it was one word:

**Choice.**

Milo did not understand.

Not yet.

But he knew they had reached the moment his parents had feared.

The moment when he would have to choose what his power was for.
