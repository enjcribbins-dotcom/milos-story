# The Comeback

The group had lost ground.

Milo began to wonder whether courage could ever be enough.

Then Nova appeared.

She did not arrive as a friend.

She arrived as a rival.

“You're going the wrong way,” she said.

Milo stared at her.

“Who are you?”

“Someone who knows what Umbra is doing.”

Nova showed them another route through the mountains.

It led to an ancient chamber beneath the stone.

There they found evidence that the relics could be used together—not simply to create more power, but to balance it.

Milo understood.

They did not need to become stronger than Umbra.

They needed to stand together.

Nova looked at him.

“You might actually have a chance.”

Milo smiled.

“I'll take that as a compliment.”

For the first time in days, the group had a plan.

But Black Paw remained silent.

Milo knew his secret was connected to the journey ahead.
