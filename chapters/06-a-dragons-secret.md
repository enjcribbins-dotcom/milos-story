# A Dragon's Secret

The strange roar stayed in Milo's thoughts.

That evening, Ember refused to settle.

He kept pacing beside the fire.

Finally he went to the pack Milo had brought home from the cave.

He sniffed it.

Then he pawed at the cloth that had covered the egg.

Milo looked up.

“What is it?”

Ember scratched at the fabric.

Beneath it was a small fragment of the egg's shell.

Milo picked it up.

The same strange markings crossed its surface.

Black Paw came closer.

Milo turned the fragment over.

“I've seen this before.”

He remembered the cave.

The symbols on the walls.

The warmth in the stone.

And the way the egg had begun to glow when they entered.

Ember touched the shell with one claw.

A faint light appeared.

Milo's breath caught.

The light formed a pattern in the air.

For only a moment, Milo saw the outline of trees, mountains and a winding path.

Then it vanished.

“Did you see that?”

Black Paw had seen it.

Milo knew because the wolf was staring at the empty air.

The next morning they returned to the cave.

The tracks had disappeared.

But the symbols remained.

Ember walked toward the largest marking.

When he touched it, the wall shimmered.

A line of light travelled through the stone.

Milo stepped closer.

The pattern was a map.

At its centre was a symbol Milo did not understand.

Beyond it lay a forest much deeper than the one around his cabin.

Milo studied the map.

“It isn't showing us where you came from,” he said slowly. “It's showing us where you're meant to go.”

Ember chirped.

Black Paw stared at the ancient symbol.

Milo suddenly understood something.

The egg had not been hidden there by accident.

Whatever had created the markings had left a path for Ember to follow when he hatched.

And somehow, Ember had known how to wake it.

“The Ancient Forest,” Milo guessed.

Ember chirped again.

Milo looked at him.

“You know it?”

The dragon lowered his head.

Milo thought about his parents.

Adventurers.

Always searching.

Always following clues that other people ignored.

He looked at the map again.

“Maybe this is what they were looking for.”

Black Paw gave him a warning look.

Milo knew what it meant.

It could be dangerous.

“I know.”

He folded the map into his pack.

“But if we don't go, we'll never know.”

Ember spread his wings.

Black Paw sighed.

The decision was made.

The three friends turned toward home.

Beyond the trees, the Ancient Forest waited.
