# The Rise of Umbra

The portal remained open above the mountains.

A figure appeared within it.

Umbra.

Shadow moved around him like smoke.

“You carry the blood of the old Guardians,” he said.

Milo's heart raced. “What do you want?”

“What your parents tried to keep from me.”

Black Paw stepped in front of Milo.

Umbra looked at the wolf.

“Still protecting him.”

Milo stared at Black Paw. “How does he know you?”

Black Paw did not answer.

Verdant and Azuren joined together, forcing Umbra back toward the portal.

“The relics belong to the one who understands their power,” Umbra said.

The darkness closed.

“Soon this world will remember me.”

Milo looked at Black Paw.

“I want the truth.”
