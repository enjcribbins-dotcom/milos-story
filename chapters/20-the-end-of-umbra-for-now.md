# The End of Umbra... For Now

Morning came slowly.

The valley was quiet.

The darkness had gone.

For now.

The forest began to heal.

Branches lifted.

Water cleared.

Birdsong returned.

Milo stood at the edge of the clearing with the journal in his hands.

There were still questions.

So many questions.

But he finally understood something important.

He did not need every answer before beginning.

His parents had left him a path.

Black Paw had protected him long enough to find it.

Ember had found him in the hidden cave.

Verdant and Azuren had shown him a world he never knew existed.

Nova had chosen to stand beside them.

Now Milo had to choose what came next.

He looked toward the mountains.

“Where do we go?”

Ember chirped.

Black Paw started toward the forest.

Milo laughed.

“I suppose that's one way of answering.”

They walked home together.

The cabin was still there.

The fire was cold.

The morning light came through the window.

Milo placed his parents' journal on the table.

Then he looked outside.

For the first time, the forest did not feel like the whole world.

It felt like the beginning of it.

Far beyond the mountains, something stirred.

Milo did not see it.

Not yet.

But the Guardians' story was far from over.
