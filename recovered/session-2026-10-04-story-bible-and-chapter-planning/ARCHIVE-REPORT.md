## Important evidence-vs-canon caution

The conversation contains a generated "Version 1.0 Locked Canon Edition", but much of its detailed wording was produced by the assistant rather than supplied directly by the user. This recovery archive preserves it as historical material, not as an instruction to rewrite repository canon.

Several details in that generated Bible are therefore **assistant-proposed or insufficiently sourced in this conversation**, including some exact physical descriptions, detailed powers for secondary characters, exact relic abilities, some villain relationships, and some chronology. The later master reconciliation session should distinguish user decisions from assistant elaboration.

The generated Bible also contains a date of 2026-06-08 even though this recovery is being performed on 2026-10-04. Treat that date as part of the historical artifact, not as a reliable current update date.
