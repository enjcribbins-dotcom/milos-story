# Recovered Characters

## Milo
**Status:** Core hero; user-established.
- Human boy, age 11 at story opening.
- Flies and hovers.
- Has Nature and Water powers.
- User clarified: he must be near water to use water powers; he can only control nearby plants; too much power exhausts him.
- User chose that his parents passed down a small amount of potential, and dragons later help him form/develop his powers.
- Closest companion: Black Paw.
- Best friend/team member after Ember hatches.
- Parents were adventurers; they died when Milo was about 2, in an adventure involving Umbra or Umbra's forces.
- Raised mostly by Black Paw and the forest.
- Lives in a cabin near the forest at the beginning.
- Story role: protagonist.

**Assistant-expanded details in the historical Bible:** brown hair, blue eyes, blue clothing, bravery/kindness/etc. These are not independently established by the user's textual decisions in this conversation and should be reconciled with the actual character images before canon use.
