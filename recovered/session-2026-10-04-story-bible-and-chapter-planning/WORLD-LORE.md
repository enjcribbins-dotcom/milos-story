# Recovered World Lore

## Magic: Milo
User-established limitations:
- Water magic requires Milo to be near water.
- Milo can only control nearby plants.
- Overuse exhausts him.
- Flight/hovering were identified as abilities; the assistant later proposed that they also consume energy, but that specific extension was not explicitly user-confirmed.
- User chose that Milo inherits a small amount of potential from his parents and dragons help him develop it.

## Elemental Guardian Relics
User chose:
- Multiple elemental relics.
- Then specifically chose one relic for every major element/dragon: eight total.

Historical assistant list:
1. Nature Relic — Verdant
2. Water Relic — Azuren
3. Fire Relic — Ember's element
4. Lightning Relic — Voltaris
5. Ice Relic — Glaciera
6. Earth Relic — Terragorn
7. Star Relic — Nova
8. Shadow Relic — Umbra

The assistant also proposed specific relic powers. Those powers were not separately confirmed by the user in the visible exchange and should be treated as historical proposals.

## Ancient Guardian War
User chose the ancient-war option:
- The Guardians and Umbra fought a war long before Milo's birth.
- The relics existed during that era.
- The relics were hidden.
- A long peace followed.
- Milo's story occurs generations later.

The exact outcome of the ancient war was not firmly resolved by the user. The assistant suggested Umbra was defeated but not destroyed; retain that as a proposal/uncertainty rather than settled fact.

## Portals
Portals become important in the chapter plan:
- Chapter 11: The Portal Opening
- Chapter 17: The Reopening of the Portal
The assistant also associated portals with Umbra's powers, but exact rules/mechanics were not established by the user.

## Locations
- Forest cabin near the forest: Milo and Black Paw's home at the beginning.
- Ancient Forest: destination in the chapter plan.
- A hidden cave: assistant-generated Chapter 2 ending hook.
- Other relic/realm locations were not concretely defined in this conversation.

## Factions
### Guardians
Milo, Black Paw, Ember, Verdant, Azuren; Nova later joins.

### Shadow Legion
Umbra plus a core of loyal shadow agents. The user selected a mixed structure where some villains are allies rather than loyal members.

### Goblin Tribe
Independent group; explicitly removed from Shadow Legion by the user.

### Independent powers
Terragorn and Inferno were placed here in the historical planning.

## Family mystery
Black Paw secretly protects a journal belonging to Milo's parents. It is intended to be revealed when Milo is ready.

The assistant's later outline says the journal contains adventure records, personal writings, ancient discoveries, relic clues, and information about Umbra. Those contents are historical planning details, not all explicit user decisions.
