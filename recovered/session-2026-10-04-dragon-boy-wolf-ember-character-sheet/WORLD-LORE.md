# World Lore

## Setting

- A valley surrounded by snowy mountains.
- A forest near the valley.
- A hidden cave where Ember is found.
- Snowy mountain terrain.
- The story spans autumn into winter and then years afterward.

## Creatures

### Dragons
- Dragons exist.
- At least Ember can speak.
- Ember can fly.
- Ember has powerful wings.
- Ember can use dragon fire according to the character-sheet material.
- Dragons can grow significantly over time.

### Wolves
- Wolves exist.
- The featured wolf is large, gray, and golden-eyed.
- The featured wolf can speak in the story.
- The wolf displays intelligence and a strong sense of reciprocal kindness.

## Magic / fantasy rules

The story establishes fantasy elements but does not define a formal magic system.

Explicitly evidenced:
- Talking dragon.
- Talking wolf.
- Dragon fire in character-sheet material.
- Exceptional dragon flight.
- No formal elemental rules are given.

## Themes

- Friendship.
- Courage.
- Loyalty.
- Kindness.
- Protecting friends.
- Returning kindness.

## Important objects

- The fallen rock trapping Ember is a plot obstacle, not a named magical object.
- No relics, artifacts, portals, or magical weapons are established.

## Terminology

- The Dragon, the Boy, and the Wolf.
