# Characters

## Milo

**Role:** Human boy; central character of the standalone story.

**Appearance:** Not described in detail in the prose.

**Personality / behavior evidenced in story:**
- Curious.
- Brave enough to help a trapped dragon despite fear.
- Kind toward animals.
- Explores the forest.
- Follows the wolf during the blizzard.

**Relationships:**
- Ember: best/secret friend.
- Wolf: rescued/guided companion who becomes part of the friendship.

**History evidenced here:**
- Milo has been leaving food in the forest during harsh winters.
- This prior kindness is connected to the wolf's decision to help him.

**Uncertainty:** No wider Milo backstory or connection to Guardians of the Elements is established in this conversation.

## Leo

**Role:** Original name for the same boy in Version A.

**Revision status:** Explicitly replaced by Milo in Version B. Retained here only as historical evidence of the earlier version.

## Ember

**Role:** Young dragon and Milo's friend.

**Prose appearance:**
- Emerald scales.
- Bright silver eyes.
- One wing is trapped beneath a fallen rock when first encountered.
- Grows larger over the years.

**Prose personality/behavior:**
- Friendly and conversational.
- Teaches Milo about stars.
- Protective and brave.
- Responds to the wolf's cry.
- Uses his wings to shield Milo and the wolf.

**Character-sheet material (assistant-generated, not independently established canon):**
- Male.
- Kind-hearted.
- Loyal.
- Curious.
- Brave.
- Playful/mischievous.
- Wise beyond his years.
- Protective.
- Likes stargazing, learning, flying, spending time with Milo and the wolf, and roasting marshmallows.
- Dislikes bullies/hunters, seeing friends upset, thunderstorms while flying, and dishonesty.
- Abilities named: Dragon Fire, Sky Dance, Star Sense, Protective Wings.
- Weaknesses include trusting too easily, stubbornness, learning to control strength, and fear of losing friends.
- A character-sheet quote was supplied: "The strongest treasure a dragon can guard is friendship."

**Visual revisions:**

- The second generated image labels Ember **The Fourflame Dragon**; this nickname is assistant-generated visual text, not a confirmed user decision.

## The Wolf

**Role:** Companion / helper.

**Appearance:**
- Large.
- Gray.
- Golden eyes.
- Later has a silver muzzle with age.

**Abilities/behavior:**
- Tracks or finds Milo during a blizzard.
- Guides Milo through snow.
- Howls to signal danger.
- Speaks one key line: kindness should be returned.

**Relationships:**
- Helps Milo.
- Trusted by Ember.
- Becomes one of the three enduring friends.

**Name:** None supplied.

## Character relationship triangle

The story develops a simple three-way friendship:
- Milo and Ember become secret friends after Milo frees Ember.
- The wolf helps Milo when he is lost.
- Ember protects Milo and the wolf during the avalanche.
- The three remain friends for years.
