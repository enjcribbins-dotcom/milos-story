## Story: The Dragon, the Boy, and the Wolf

### VERSION A — Original boy name: Leo

> Long ago, in a valley surrounded by snowy mountains, there lived a boy named Leo. He was curious about everything and often wandered farther into the forest than anyone else dared.
>
> One autumn afternoon, while following a trail of golden leaves, Leo heard a strange sound. It was not a bird, nor the wind. It was a low, rumbling cry.
>
> He followed the sound to a hidden cave.
>
> Inside lay a young dragon with emerald scales and bright silver eyes. One of its wings was trapped beneath a fallen rock.
>
> Leo's heart pounded. He had heard stories about dragons, but he had never seen one.
>
> "Are you going to eat me?" Leo asked.
>
> The dragon blinked.
>
> "No," it replied. "I'm stuck."
>
> Leo stared. "You can talk?"
>
> "So can you," said the dragon.
>
> For a moment, neither of them spoke. Then Leo laughed.
>
> Together, they worked to free the dragon. It took hours, but at last the rock rolled away.
>
> "I'm Ember," said the dragon.
>
> "I'm Leo."
>
> From that day on, they became secret friends.
>
> Every week, Leo visited the cave. Ember taught him about the stars, and Leo taught Ember about people. Their friendship grew stronger with each passing season.
>
> One winter evening, however, trouble arrived.
>
> A fierce blizzard swept across the mountains. Leo was returning home when he lost the path. Snow whirled around him until he could barely see his own feet.
>
> Soon he realized he was not alone.
>
> A large gray wolf emerged from the storm.
>
> Leo froze.
>
> The wolf's golden eyes watched him carefully.
>
> Then the wolf turned and began walking away.
>
> After a few steps, it looked back.
>
> "You want me to follow you?" Leo wondered.
>
> The wolf barked once.
>
> Having no better choice, Leo followed.
>
> The wolf led him through the snow, but before they reached safety, a terrible crack echoed across the mountain.
>
> An avalanche.
>
> A wall of snow rushed toward them.
>
> Leo stumbled.
>
> The wolf howled.
>
> Far above, Ember heard the cry.
>
> The dragon burst from his cave and soared into the storm.
>
> He spotted Leo and the wolf just as the avalanche thundered down the slope.
>
> With a mighty roar, Ember landed before them and spread his enormous wings. Snow crashed against him like ocean waves against a cliff.
>
> For a moment, everything disappeared in white.
>
> Then silence.
>
> When the snow settled, Leo, Ember, and the wolf were safe.
>
> The three sat together beneath the moonlight.
>
> "You saved us," Leo said to Ember.
>
> The dragon smiled.
>
> "The wolf found you first."
>
> Leo looked at the wolf. "Why did you help me?"
>
> The wolf tilted its head.
>
> "Because," it said softly, "kindness should be returned."
>
> Leo remembered leaving food in the forest during harsh winters for hungry animals. He had never known this wolf was among them.
>
> That night, the three friends returned to the valley together.
>
> Years passed. Leo grew older, Ember grew larger, and the wolf's muzzle turned silver with age. Yet their friendship never faded.
>
> People often spoke of a mysterious dragon in the mountains, a wise wolf in the woods, and a brave young man who seemed to understand them both.
>
> Most thought the stories were legends.
>
> But whenever the moon shone brightly over the valley, three shadows could still be seen crossing the snow together—a dragon, a boy, and a wolf, united by friendship, courage, and kindness.
