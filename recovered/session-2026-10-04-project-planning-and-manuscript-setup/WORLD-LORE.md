# Recovered World Lore

- Adventure + mystery was selected as the preferred tone/direction.
- The story is associated with the title **Guardians of the Elements**.

- Dragons are central to the story.
- An ancient forest is a location.
- A portal is part of the story.
- The title and project context indicate elemental guardians as part of the wider world.
- Verdant and Azuren are named in the chapter list.

## Core-context separation
Character sheets and world notes are intended to live outside the clean novel manuscript as the project's core context/story bible.

No additional magic rules, mythology, history, factions, artifacts, or portal mechanics are asserted because they are not available in this conversation context.
